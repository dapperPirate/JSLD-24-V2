JACK SHORTER

Lighting Programmer & Designer

Jack Shorter is a Lighting Programmer & Designer based in South East England. His work is primarily focused within the theatrical world, but is always looking for opportunities to work outside of traditional boundaries. He particularly enjoys working in unconventional environments.

Jack's body of work ranges from musicals performed in regional venues to intimate black box productions and plays performed in retrofit barns in the Hampshire countryside.

His blog contains articles about things that personally interest him, and are at least somewhat vaguely related to theatre.
