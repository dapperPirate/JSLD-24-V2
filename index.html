JACK SHORTER

Lighting Designer & Programmer

Jack Shorter is a Lighting Designer & Programmer based in Surrey and Hampshire. His work is primarily focused within the theatrical world, but is always looking for opportunities to work outside of traditional boundaries.

Jacks body of work ranges from musicals performed in regional venues to intimate black box productions and plays performed in retrofit barns in the Hampshire countryside.

He can be contacted on this email address: jack@jackshorterlightingdesign.co.uk
